Your data, plainly.

No ads, no selling, no tracking tools. Here is what leaves your phone and why. The full version is in the privacy policy.

Photos go to the AI, once

A meal photo is sent to an AI service to estimate it. Photos you don’t log are deleted within a day. Progress photos never go to the AI.

Your voice stays on your phone

Say it uses your phone’s speech recognition. Plato gets the words, never the audio.

Delete everything, any time

Settings → Account → Delete account erases your logs, photos and conversations straight away. More ways to delete.

Delete your Plato account

How to erase your account and everything in it.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Plato and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is erased straight away.

Without the app

Email [support email] from the Google account you use with Plato, with the subject “Delete my Plato account”. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

Everything in your account, permanently: your profile and goals, food logs, meal photos, progress photos, weigh-ins, workouts, water, saved meals and recipes, coach conversations and tips. This can’t be undone.

Nothing is kept for later. Photos of scans you didn’t log are already deleted within about 24 hours.

Your subscription

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Google keeps its own purchase records.

More about your data is in the privacy policy.

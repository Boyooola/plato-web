Privacy policy

Plato: AI calorie and nutrition tracker · Last updated 27 November 2026

The short version. Plato keeps your food logs, weigh-ins, photos and settings so it can track your eating for you. It sends meal photos and descriptions to an AI service to estimate nutrition, and your recent logs to the same service when the coach answers you. It has no ads, does not sell your data, and does not use analytics or tracking tools. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time.

This policy explains what Plato (“we”, “us”) collects, why, who helps us process it, and your choices. Questions: support@useplato.app.

What we collect

Your account

  • When you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile photo link. We never see your Google password.

What you tell Plato

  • Profile and goals: sex, date of birth, height, weight, goal weight, activity level, weekly pace, unit system, and the calorie and macro targets worked out from them.
  • Food logs: meals, foods, amounts, nutrients and times, plus the text you type or say when describing a meal.
  • Meal photos you take or choose to log a meal. Photos that never become a logged meal (retaken or abandoned scans) are deleted automatically after about 24 hours.
  • Progress photos, weigh-ins, workouts you log, water, and saved meals and recipes.
  • Coach conversations and the daily tips the coach writes for you.
  • Problem reports and feedback you choose to send: what went wrong, an optional note, and your app and Android versions. If you report a wrong food estimate or coach answer, the report also includes what Plato found or said, as text (never your photo).

Health Connect (only if you turn it on)

  • With your permission, Plato reads your steps, active calories burned and weight from Health Connect. It never writes to Health Connect.
  • We use this data only to show your activity on Today and Progress, to add exercise to your daily goal if you choose that, to record weigh-ins, and to give the coach your activity when it answers you or writes a tip.
  • We don’t use Health Connect data for advertising, don’t sell it, and don’t share it except with the service providers below, only to provide these features. You can revoke access at any time in Health Connect’s settings.

Voice

  • When you use Say it, your phone’s speech recognition service (for example Google’s) turns your words into text. Plato never receives your audio, only the text, which you can edit before anything is sent.

Purchases

  • Subscriptions are bought through Google Play. We receive your subscription status (for example, active until a date), never your card details. A subscription management service keeps that status in sync, linked to your Plato account ID.

Stored only on your phone

  • App settings such as appearance, meal reminders, and whether you hid today’s tip. The home-screen widget shows today’s numbers and is cleared when you sign out.

We don’t collect your location, contacts or advertising ID, and we don’t use analytics, crash-tracking or advertising tools.

On our website

  • If you join the early testers at useplato.app, we keep the email address you enter, only to invite you to the test and tell you when Plato launches. Ask us to delete it any time.
  • Like any website, useplato.app is served by a hosting provider and loads fonts from a font provider, which receive standard request data such as your IP address. The site uses no cookies or analytics.

How we use it

  • To run the app: your diary, targets, streaks, progress, reminders and the widget.
  • To estimate nutrition from photos, labels and descriptions, and to answer your coach questions and write tips, using AI (see below).
  • To manage your subscription and free-use limits.
  • To fix problems you report.

We don’t use your data for advertising, and we don’t sell it or share it for others’ marketing.

AI processing

Plato uses an AI service provider to:

  • estimate food and nutrients from meal photos, nutrition labels and descriptions;
  • answer your coach questions and write daily tips. For these, it sends your profile and targets, about 14 days of food totals, today’s meals, frequent foods, weigh-ins and activity (including Health Connect steps and active calories, if connected).

The request goes from our server to the AI provider, with the name you go by in the app but never your email, and the answer comes back to you. The provider processes it on our behalf, only to return the answer. Progress photos are never sent to the AI. AI estimates can be wrong, so check amounts that matter to you.

Where it’s stored and how it’s protected

Your data is stored in Japan and may be processed in other countries by the providers above, under data protection terms with each of them. It travels encrypted (HTTPS). Each account can reach only its own rows and files, and photos are in private storage that is shown to you through short-lived links.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. Unlogged scan photos are deleted after about 24 hours. When you delete your account, your data is erased as described below.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently erases your account, profile, food logs, meal and progress photos, weigh-ins, workouts, coach conversations and tips straight away. It can’t be undone.

Without the app: email support@useplato.app from the Google account you use with Plato, with the subject “Delete my Plato account”. We’ll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Google Play keeps its own purchase records.

You can also ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, at the same address.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. Email us to use any of these rights. We process your data to provide the service you asked for (contract) and, for health data such as Health Connect, with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Children

Plato is not meant for anyone under 16, and we don’t knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has an account, email us and we’ll delete it.

Not medical advice

Plato’s numbers, estimates, tips and coach answers are general information, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian about medical conditions, pregnancy, or concerns about eating.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
